# Printed Representation (October 2026)

Checked on 2026-10-31. All sources were read that day: resolutions and annexes on sunat.gob.pe and ww3.sunat.gob.pe, SUNAT orientation pages (orientacion.sunat.gob.pe), SUNAT's "Consulta de Validez del CPE" page, and the validation workbook in `sources/sunat/reglas-validacion-2026-08-26/`. Anything marked *unconfirmed* is not stated by an official source.

Scope: the Printed Representation of an Invoice, Receipt, Credit Note and Debit Note issued from SEE-Del Contribuyente (R.S. 097-2012/SUNAT). The SEE-OSE rules (R.S. 117-2017/SUNAT) are cited only where they confirm or fill in a point.

### How to read the annex columns

| Column | Meaning (annex notes) |
|---|---|
| Condición de emisión (C) | "Elementos constitutivos del documento electrónico, por lo que su incumplimiento tiene como consecuencia la inexistencia del mismo": without it, no Document exists. Annexes 1, 3 and 4 only |
| Requisito mínimo (R) | A minimum requirement of the Document. Any field "de utilización obligatoria" also counts as a requisito mínimo (note 4 of Annex 1, note 3 of Annex 2) |
| Representación impresa – información mínima del resumen (P) | Must appear on the Printed Representation. Annexes 3 and 4 have one P column for notes that modify an Invoice and another for notes that modify a Receipt |

**Method.** The X marks were read by their position on the page with a PDF library, because plain text extraction loses the columns. The column positions match the header of each annex. Two rows in the 2022 Annex 1 PDF were checked against the original R.S. 193-2020 Annex I: items 49-A, 64-A and 64-B.

## 2. Mandatory content per document type

Legend:

- **M**: in the P column, always.
- **C**: in the P column, but the annex says "solo de corresponder" or the field applies only in some cases. Print it when the Document has the data.
- **—**: not in the P column. The field may still be required in the XML.

Item numbers are the annex numbers: F = Annex 1, B = Annex 2, NC = Annex 3, ND = Annex 4.

**Detracción outside the annexes.** R.S. 183-2004 art. 19.1 b) requires the phrase "Operación sujeta al Sistema de Pago de Obligaciones Tributarias con el Gobierno Central", "como información no necesariamente impresa". See [detracciones-2026-10.md](detracciones-2026-10.md) § 4. For an Invoice, catalog 52 code `2006` is its electronic form. The workbook gives OBSERV 4265 when operation type `1001`–`1004` has no `2006` legend (`factura-2.0.csv`, item 57). Printing the account number or the amount is not required by the annex (*unconfirmed* whether R.S. 183-2004 requires it elsewhere on the Document).

**Payment Terms on paper.** The Invoice annex does not put Payment Terms, the pending amount or the Installments in the P column. They are conditions of issue for the XML. Printing them is allowed, but no official text found requires it (*unconfirmed*).

**Amount in words.** It exists only as optional legend `1000`. No workbook rule requires it: no rule forces a `1000` legend in `factura-2.0.csv`. Its P mark is the generic "solo de corresponder" of F61.

## 4. The legend and the consultation URL

- **Wording prescribed by the annexes** (P column, "Solo en la representación impresa"):
  - Invoice (F62): "Representación impresa de la factura electrónica"
  - Receipt (B58): "Representación impresa de la boleta de venta electrónica"
  - Credit Note (NC45): "Representación impresa de la nota de crédito electrónica"
  - Debit Note (ND45): "Representación impresa de la nota de débito electrónica"
- **The annexes prescribe no URL.** Field 62 and its peers have no "consulte en …" text.
- **SUNAT's consultation.** R.S. 097-2012 art. 27 has SUNAT offer, through SUNAT Virtual, a check of "la validez así como la información de las condiciones de emisión y requisitos mínimos" of Invoices and their notes. For Receipts and their notes, the check verifies the minimum requirements sent, or whether the Daily Summary reported them and on what date. The public page is "Consulta de Validez del Comprobante de Pago Electrónico", e-consulta.sunat.gob.pe/ol-ti-itconsvalicpe/ConsValiCpe.htm (reachable on 2026-10-31).
- **Printing a URL.** Printing SUNAT's URL or the Issuer's own site is not required by any text found. It is not forbidden either (*unconfirmed*).
- **The Issuer's own site.** SUNAT says the Issuer must give each Receipt Customer access to their Receipts for one year from issue, through a web page, with authentication so that only that Customer can see them (orientacion.sunat.gob.pe/node/722, "05. Otorgamiento y Conservación – Boleta de Venta Electrónica desde SEE Contribuyente"). That is the natural URL to print, but the norm does not require printing it. The article number in R.S. 097-2012 is *unconfirmed*: presumably art. 25 "De la conservación y de la puesta a disposición…", whose title appears in R.S. 000048-2026.

## 6. Open questions

- Must a Receipt to an identified Customer print the Customer's identity document and name? They are a minimum requirement (B11, B12) but not in the P column.
- Are Payment Terms, the pending amount and the Installments expected on a credit Invoice's Printed Representation? Annex 1 does not mark them.
- Does a PDF sent by email, without the XML, satisfy delivery "por medios electrónicos"?
- Is there an amendment to Annexes 2–4 between 2019 and 2026 that was not found? The missing ICBPER rows suggest there is one.
- What is the current exact wording of the first paragraph of art. 7.4 d)? The last version read (R.S. 065-2013) names the valor resumen and the barcode of Annex 6 numerals 6.2 and 6.3. The QR paragraph was added later.

## What a template must show

The list below is the strict minimum (M) plus the fields to show when the data exists (C).

**Every Document**

- Issuer name or razón social. The nombre comercial if the Issuer has one.
- "RUC" followed by the Issuer's RUC.
- The title as text: "Factura electrónica", "Boleta de venta electrónica", "Nota de crédito electrónica" or "Nota de débito electrónica". On notes it goes before the Series-Number.
- Series-Number.
- Issue date.
- Currency sign or name.
- For each Item:
  - unit of measure (may be omitted on Receipts for NIU or ZZ);
  - quantity (may be omitted on Receipts when it is 1);
  - description, in Spanish;
  - Unit Price.
- Sales totals by kind: gravadas, inafectas, exoneradas, exportación, and gratuitas on Invoices and Receipts. Show each when present.
- Document total.
- Catalog 52 legends the Document carries (Invoice and Receipt), such as the detracción legend and the amount in words.
- The legend "Representación impresa de la …" for its type.
- The QR at the bottom: at most 6×6 cm including a quiet zone of at least 1 mm, black, level Q, UTF-8, the nine-field pipe string with a trailing pipe.
- If thermal paper is used, paper legible for at least one year.

**Invoice, in addition**

- Customer identity document, by name and number, and the Customer's name.
- IGV/IVAP, ISC, ICBPER and other taxes when present.
- Other charges, other discounts and global charges or discounts when present.
- Rounding when present.
- Dispatch guide and other related documents when present.
- Vehicle plate and itinerant delivery address when applicable.

**Receipt, in addition**

- Item Value and Item tax total.
- Total sales value, subtotal.
- Other discounts and global charges or discounts when present.
- Related document or code when present.
- Customer identity: not in the annex's printed column (see Unconfirmed).

**Credit Note and Debit Note, in addition**

- Customer identity document and name.
- The Reason text ("Motivo").
- The Affected Document's type and Series-Number.
- On notes that modify an Invoice: Unit Value, IGV/IVAP, ISC, other taxes, other charges and total taxes, when present.
- On a Debit Note that modifies a Receipt: the Reason code.
- On a Debit Note: a related dispatch guide or document when present.

**Not required on paper:** domicilio fiscal, establishment address or code, Customer address, operation type, Product Codes, Payment Terms and Installments, detracción account and amount, hash, PDF417, any URL.

## Unconfirmed

- Whether RCP (R.S. 007-99) art. 8 adds printed requirements for electronic Documents.
- Whether a Receipt to an identified Customer must print the Customer's identity document and name.
- Whether Payment Terms, the net pending amount and the Installments must be printed on a credit Invoice.
- Whether R.S. 183-2004 requires printing the detracción account or amount, beyond the legend.
- Whether the amount in words is mandatory. No annex or workbook rule requires it.
- Whether printing the hash (valor resumen) after 2020 is allowed. No text forbids it.
- Whether printing SUNAT's consultation URL or the Issuer's URL is allowed or expected. No text requires it.
- Whether a PDF emailed without the XML counts as delivery by electronic means.
- The SEE-Del Contribuyente equivalents of R.S. 117-2017 art. 18.4 (when a Document is available) and art. 31.2 (new Printed Representation after a loss).
- The R.S. 097-2012 article that sets the one-year web access for Receipts. Presumably art. 25.
- The current first paragraph of art. 7.4 d).
- Whether an amendment to Annexes 2–4 issued between 2019 and 2026 was missed. It is likely: the 2019 Annexes 2–4 have no ICBPER rows, while Annex 1 (2022) has them (F25-A, F40-A).
